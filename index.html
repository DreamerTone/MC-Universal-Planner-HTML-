Where do I find a vanilla .jar?

Vanilla client jars live in your Minecraft installation:

  • Windows: %appdata%\.minecraft\versions\<version>\<version>.jar
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/versions/<version>/<version>.jar
  • Linux: ~/.minecraft/versions/<version>/<version>.jar

Use the client jar (not the server jar) so block textures are included. Files stay on your device — nothing is uploaded.

Classification

The planner currently guesses categories from registry ids, tags, models, and recipe shapes. Unknown modded content is kept visible instead of hidden, then can be refined into custom rules later.
